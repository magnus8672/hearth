03 / Thoughtfully connected

A shared home.
Your own space.

Go beyond running models. Bring a growing collection of capabilities together in one useful, personal system.

One place to shape it

Connect model services, assign capabilities and manage your machines in one place. Guided setup and managed installation make growing the farm simpler.

Trust, built in

Private workspaces, encrypted connections and multi-factor sign-in protect your home cloud. Verified workers and tightly scoped tool permissions keep access under your control.

Room for everyone

Separate accounts and personal conversations, with shared channels when you want company. Clear boundaries between using the farm and administering it.

A lighter job per machine

Choose focused models that fit your available memory. Give older hardware a useful role in tools, storage or knowledge services.

Capabilities that compose

Bring writing, code, images, voice, vision and 3D into the same workflow. Shared tools and memory give your specialists more ways to work together.

Local is the starting point

Keep work on your own systems. Use optional cloud assistance with explicit permission and budgets, while local-only work stays local throughout.
